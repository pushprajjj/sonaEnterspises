ABOUT

Established in 1991, Sona Enterprises is a trusted name in the electrical goods manufacturing industry. With over three decades of expertise, we specialize in producing high-quality immersion rods, cooler pumps, ceiling fans, madhanis, motors, stators, AP fans, and spare parts such as cooler blades and connectors.
With a strong distribution network and a customer-first approach, we continue to power homes and businesses with excellence.
